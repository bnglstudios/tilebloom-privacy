Privacy Policy

TileBloom: Picture Puzzle · Last updated 6 September 2026

  • Türkçe

TileBloom is published by BNGL Studios (“we”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not collect your name or email, and we never upload your photos. The only personal data involved is the advertising identifier your device gives to our advertising partner, Google AdMob.

Your photos stay on your device

TileBloom lets you turn a photo from your phone into a puzzle. When you choose a photo, a copy is made inside the app’s own private storage, so the puzzle still works if you later move or delete the original. That copy never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, not backed up to us, and not shared with anyone. When you delete a custom puzzle, the copy is deleted with it. Uninstalling the app removes all of them.

We use the Android system photo picker, which means the app is never granted access to your photo library as a whole — only to the single picture you pick.

Your progress stays on your device

Levels solved, coins, diamonds, badges and settings are stored in a file inside the app. We cannot see it. There is no cloud save and no account, so uninstalling the app deletes your progress permanently.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, AdMob receives your device’s advertising identifier and technical information such as your device model, operating system version and approximate location derived from your IP address. It also records how you interact with the ads themselves — whether one was shown, tapped or watched to the end — and basic diagnostics about how the app performs, such as how long it takes to start. We do not receive any of this ourselves; we receive only aggregate reports about how many ads were shown.

Google’s own policy explains what they do with it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalised advertising, using Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

You can also limit ad tracking on the device itself, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Children

TileBloom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

What we do not do

We have added no analytics or crash reporting of our own, so nothing about how you play reaches us. We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell data. There are no in-app purchases, so we never handle payment details.

Permissions

The app requests internet access, which is needed to load ads, and on Android 9 and older the permission to save a finished picture to your gallery when you ask it to. It requests no other permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date at the top.

Bir fotoğraf seçtiğinizde, uygulamanın kendi özel alanına bir kopyası alınır; böylece orijinali sonradan taşısanız veya silseniz de bulmaca çalışmaya devam eder. Bu kopya cihazınızdan hiç çıkmaz. Yüklenmez, bize yedeklenmez, kimseyle paylaşılmaz. Bir bulmacayı sildiğinizde kopyası da silinir. Uygulamayı kaldırdığınızda hepsi silinir.

Android’in sistem fotoğraf seçicisini kullanıyoruz; bu, uygulamanın fotoğraf kitaplığınızın tamamına değil, yalnızca seçtiğiniz tek resme erişmesi demektir.
